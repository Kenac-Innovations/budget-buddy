# AI assistants and copilots

Status: DRAFT, awaiting Kenac review
Review date: 2026-10-14 (draft preparation; no Kenac sign-off)
Next review: 2027-01-14, or sooner if source guidance changes

## What it is
Generative AI assistance needs task-specific evaluation and controls for information handling. The NIST generative AI profile describes risks and actions for managing them; it does not promise a productivity gain.

## Who it fits in Zimbabwe
Planning judgement: pilot a small, willing role group on low-risk tasks before buying organisation-wide licences. Keep existing workflows where benefits are unproven. Agree a measured success criterion and stop condition.

## When it is hype or premature, and prerequisites
Premature: a blanket rollout based on a sales demonstration or autonomous work on sensitive decisions. Prerequisites: permission boundaries, approved data handling, human review and a baseline for time and quality.

## Common failures
Common failures to check: inaccurate outputs used without review, confidential material exposed, pilots without measurement and subscriptions left running after abandonment. Include training and evaluation effort.

## Questions to ask
Which repetitive task is suitable for a controlled pilot? What improvement would justify continuing?

## Sources with dates
[NIST AI risk management framework and generative AI profile](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), profile released 2024-07-26, accessed 2026-10-14; risk guidance, not proof of local productivity benefits.
