# Security baseline (MFA, EDR, patching; CIS IG1 to IG2)

Status: DRAFT, awaiting Kenac review
Review date: 2026-10-14 (draft preparation; no Kenac sign-off)
Next review: 2027-01-14, or sooner if source guidance changes

## What it is
CIS implementation groups prioritise safeguards according to resources and risk. IG1 is essential hygiene; IG2 builds on it. A tool purchase alone does not establish a functioning control.

## Who it fits in Zimbabwe
Planning judgement: assess asset ownership, access controls, patching and recovery evidence first, then investigate endpoint detection and response with a named response owner. Keep effective controls; fund verified gaps before advanced platforms.

## When it is hype or premature, and prerequisites
Premature: moving to an advanced operations platform without baseline coverage or people to respond. Prerequisites: ownership, a risk register, practical patch windows, privileged-access control and recovery exercises.

## Common failures
Common failures to check: enrolled endpoints without response coverage, unowned alerts, unmanaged accounts and policies without implementation. Vague budget labels such as “security improvement” are sufficient; never probe vulnerabilities.

## Questions to ask
Which baseline controls have evidence of coverage? What can the existing team operate consistently?
