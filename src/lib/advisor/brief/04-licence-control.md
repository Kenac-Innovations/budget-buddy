## What it is
Usage reports can inform a licence review. Low activity is a review signal rather than proof that a licence is unnecessary; security, retention and workload requirements also matter.

## Who it fits in Zimbabwe
Planning judgement: organisations with a productivity suite should reconcile allocated licences with roles and joiner/leaver processes before expanding subscriptions. Keep the current suite where it fits; fund a controlled entitlement review.

## When it is hype or premature, and prerequisites
Premature: removing licences solely from a last-login report or upgrading everyone to an advanced tier. Prerequisites: service ownership, an entitlement inventory, retention requirements and an agreed approval process.

## Common failures
Common failures to check: removing a security control bundled in a licence, deleting retained information, ignoring seasonal users or forgetting licences for new branches. Use aggregate bands in conversation; do not request employee-level reports.

## Questions to ask
Which capabilities are actually needed by each role band? Who approves and removes access when people join or leave?
