## What it is
Zimbabwe’s data protection framework includes the Cyber and Data Protection Act and regulations addressing controller licensing and data protection officers. Applicability and current obligations need confirmation with POTRAZ and a qualified adviser.

## Who it fits in Zimbabwe
Planning judgement: investigate data ownership and handling controls without gathering personal records in this conversation. Keep effective controls; fund a scoped review of documented gaps, using a vague line name if preferred.

## When it is hype or premature, and prerequisites
Premature: declaring compliance from a software purchase or assuming a foreign certification satisfies local obligations. Prerequisites: internal ownership, a data-flow review and qualified interpretation of current requirements.

## Common failures
Common failures to check: unclear controller/processor responsibilities, excessive retention, unclear incident ownership or transfers approved without review. Do not assert deadlines, exemptions or penalties in advice.

## Questions to ask
Is someone responsible for confirming your obligations and data handling? No customer records or sensitive diagrams are needed here.
