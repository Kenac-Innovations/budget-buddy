## What it is
Standard Windows 10 version 22H2 support ended on 2025-10-14. Extended security update arrangements and other editions require separate lifecycle checks; do not assume every device is unsupported.

## Who it fits in Zimbabwe
Planning judgement: investigate remaining unsupported endpoints and incompatible applications before planning replacement. Keep serviceable supported devices; assess supported upgrades or evidenced temporary coverage alongside replacement.

## When it is hype or premature, and prerequisites
Premature: replacing the whole fleet because of age alone. Prerequisites: support status by device band, application compatibility, data migration, warranty needs and a secure disposal process.

## Common failures
Common failures to check: counting owned devices as replacement quantities, missing deployment effort, unsupported peripherals or unrecoverable local data. Include migration, training and disposal as unpriced costs.

## Questions to ask
Roughly how many devices need action in 2027? Which business applications constrain the upgrade path?
