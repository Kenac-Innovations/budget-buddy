# Multi-branch networking and SD-WAN

Status: DRAFT, awaiting Kenac review
Review date: 2026-10-14 (draft preparation; no Kenac sign-off)
Next review: 2027-01-14, or sooner if source guidance changes

## What it is
An application-aware network overlay governs traffic over underlying connectivity. MEF distinguishes the overlay service from its underlay; it does not replace physical connectivity or branch power.

## Who it fits in Zimbabwe
Planning judgement: investigate policy consistency and operations across growing branch estates. Keep current routing where it meets needs; pilot an overlay at representative sites only after documenting the operational problem.

## When it is hype or premature, and prerequisites
Premature: funding an overlay to cure power outages or merely because branches are planned. Prerequisites: confirmed branch timing, working power and connectivity, application priorities, segmentation and an operations owner.

## Common failures
Common failures to check: shared underlay failures, complex policies nobody owns, insufficient link capacity and missing recurring support. Include commissioning and monitoring as unpriced lines.

## Questions to ask
Which network problem persists when power and links are healthy? Who will maintain and test the policies?
