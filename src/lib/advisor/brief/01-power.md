# Power resilience (solar, UPS, inverters)

Status: DRAFT, awaiting Kenac review
Review date: 2026-10-14 (draft preparation; no Kenac sign-off)
Next review: 2027-01-14, or sooner if source guidance changes

## What it is
Storage and protected power keep essential services running through a grid interruption. Solar alone does not establish backup capability: the design must support operation during grid loss.

## Who it fits in Zimbabwe
Planning judgement: branches reporting power-related outages should assess critical loads before buying network upgrades. Fund assessment and protection of essential services; keep working equipment where measured runtime is adequate.

## When it is hype or premature, and prerequisites
Premature: specifying a whole-site solar system before measuring loads and required runtime. Prerequisites: a qualified electrical assessment, safe isolation, load priorities, battery condition and commissioning criteria.

## Common failures
Common failures to check: omitted network equipment, insufficient battery autonomy, unsafe wiring, missing maintenance and replacement planning. Include installation, testing and safe battery disposal as unpriced lines.

## Questions to ask
Which services must survive a cut, and for roughly how long? Has runtime been tested under load?
