# Paying for cloud and SaaS in USD (cards, transfer costs)

Status: DRAFT, awaiting Kenac review
Review date: 2026-10-14 (draft preparation; no Kenac sign-off)
Next review: 2027-01-14, or sooner if source guidance changes

## What it is
A recurring foreign-currency service needs a dependable payment route as well as an allocation. Renewal terms, billing currency and actual bank charges belong in the budget assumptions.

## Who it fits in Zimbabwe
Planning judgement: Zimbabwe organisations using overseas subscriptions should involve Finance in renewal planning. Keep a reliable current payment route; investigate resilience where failed payment could stop a critical service.

## When it is hype or premature, and prerequisites
Premature: assuming an advertised subscription amount is the full landed cost or applying remembered tax rates. Prerequisites: Finance confirmation of current bank/card capability, tax treatment and authorised payment arrangements.

## Common failures
Common failures to check: renewal on an unavailable card, missing transfer charges, unintended annual commitment or a foreign-currency line counted as converted without an exchange-rate assumption.

## Questions to ask
Who owns renewals, and has Finance confirmed the payment route and applicable charges? Ask for evidence only at the budget stage.
